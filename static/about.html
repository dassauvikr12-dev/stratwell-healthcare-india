Our story

Built around the people who keep hotels and hospitals running.

Hirezyy combines operating experience, recruitment understanding, training capability and human-led coordination.

The trigger

A talent gap seen from inside organisations.

The idea first took shape in hospitality in 2013, when mid-sized organisations were struggling to access dependable service and operations talent. From 2014 onward, the same pressure became visible in hospitals, where bulk hiring and high attrition affected operations, team continuity and patient experience.

Hirezyy was formalised in 2026 as a unit of High Value Edge to focus on these two people-intensive sectors: hotels and hospitals.

Hospitality · supply chain · people management · operational leadership

With over two decades of experience spanning hospitality and supply chain, Binoy brings people management, operational leadership and business understanding to recruitment.

A Hotel Management graduate from IHM Guwahati with an MBA in Event Management, he built experience in Food & Beverage Service, Banquet Operations and Event Management in India and the UK.

His career included Goa Marriott, Hyatt Regency Delhi, East West Food Group Birmingham UK, Viceroy Marriott Hyderabad, Eros Hilton Delhi, The Park Delhi, Radisson Blu Guwahati and Clarks Inn Suites Ghaziabad. He later spent more than a decade with Delhivery Ltd. across administration, procurement and fleet operations.

These organisations describe Binoy’s professional experience and are not presented as Hirezyy clients or endorsements.
