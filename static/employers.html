How we work

Understand. Source. Screen. Coordinate.

  1. Clarify the role

    Department, shift, service standards, compensation, location and joining timeline.

  2. Find relevant people

    Targeted sourcing rather than undirected CV forwarding.

  3. Screen for fit

    Experience, communication, motivation and workplace readiness.

  4. Keep decisions moving

    Interview coordination, feedback, offers and joining follow-up.
