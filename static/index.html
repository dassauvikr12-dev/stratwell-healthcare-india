Why Hirezyy exists

A recruitment idea shaped inside service organisations.

The founders repeatedly saw the same gap: quality people were difficult to find at the right time, while heavy attrition weakened operations, customer experience and growth.

2013

The idea began in hospitality, where mid-sized hotels and service organisations struggled to source dependable people.

2014 onward

Hospital hiring revealed the cost of bulk recruitment, high attrition and disrupted patient experience.

2026

Hirezyy was formalised as a unit of High Value Edge to connect hospital and hotel talent faster, smarter and more reliably.
