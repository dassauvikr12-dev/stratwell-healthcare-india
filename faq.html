Frequently asked questions

What would you like to know?

How do I register as a nursing candidate?

Use the candidate registration form to prepare an enquiry with your qualification, experience, professional registration and preferred locations. Send it to Dipta by email or WhatsApp. The team will discuss next steps and any documents required.

Which levels of positions can I enquire about?

Junior, mid-level, specialist and senior leadership roles across healthcare. Each introduction depends on suitable employer requirements and your eligibility.

Is Stratwell focused only on healthcare?

Yes. The talent partnership presented here focuses on healthcare organisations and healthcare professionals. Leadership experience in hospitality informs service and training approaches.

Can I apply from another state?

Yes. Tell us your current location and preferred cities or states. Relocation, employer availability, professional eligibility and terms are discussed for each opportunity.

Do I have to pay to register or get placed?

This website does not collect registration or placement payments. Ask for the written terms of any service before committing. Optional paid training, if available, must have separately agreed fees, scope and cancellation terms.

Does training guarantee a job?

No. Training supports preparation. Employer screening, vacancies, eligibility and selection determine placement.

What does screening involve?

The intended process covers qualification and applicable registration checks, relevant experience, consent-based references, role fit and location preferences. Employers may conduct further assessments.

How will I receive interview and selection updates?

Contact Dipta in your email or WhatsApp enquiry thread. Automated confirmation and selection emails are not currently active. Once an interview is confirmed, the candidate page can create a calendar reminder.

Is the form submitted automatically?

No. It prepares a message for your review. You must send it through your email app or WhatsApp. No application is delivered merely by clicking Prepare enquiry.

Can I move to another hospital later?

You can remain in touch for future suitable openings. New introductions require your consent and depend on vacancies, eligibility, employer selection and any current employment obligations.

How do employers share vacancies?

Use the employer requirements page to provide roles, seniority, location, headcount, salary and joining expectations, then send the prepared brief by email or WhatsApp.

Where is your office?

Contact Dipta to request the confirmed official address and arrange an appointment before visiting.
