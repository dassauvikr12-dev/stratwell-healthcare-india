Clear expectations

Learning, without
overpromising.

Can our institution request a customised programme?

Yes. Share your learner profile, preferred topics, group size, location and tentative dates. The team will discuss an appropriate scope before confirming availability.

Are hospital visits or clinical training included?

The programmes described here focus on career and workplace preparation. Hospital visits require separate host permission and arrangements. Any clinical training requires suitable qualified trainers and relevant approvals; it is not included by default.

Are fees or certificates confirmed on this page?

No. Programme availability, trainer details, fees, cancellation terms and any participation certificate are confirmed in writing for the specific programme. No accredited or clinical certification is promised on this page.

Does attending guarantee an internship or a job?

No. Training and recruitment are separate. Employer selection, role requirements and available opportunities determine recruitment outcomes.
