Nursing staff registration

A clear route into care teams.

  1. Introduce yourself

    Share your qualification, experience, speciality, registration status and preferred locations.

  2. Prepare your documents

    Have your CV, qualification certificates and applicable professional registration ready. Send copies only when the team requests them through an agreed channel.

  3. Complete screening

    Discuss role fit, experience, references and document checks. Employer-led clinical assessment may follow.

  4. Meet the employer

    Receive agreed interview instructions and discuss outcomes with your talent partner.

  5. Stay connected

    Keep your profile current for future opportunities in other hospitals or states, with your consent.
